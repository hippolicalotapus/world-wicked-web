# Editorial and evidence standard — World Wicked Web

Status: development policy, not a claim that published articles have been verified.

## Purpose
Document serious crime, harmful historical events and other difficult subjects for responsible research. The archive is not a celebration of offenders and does not sell access to victims' suffering.

## Source and verification requirements
- Each factual entry must include a stable title, subject category, publication/review date, at least one attributable source, and an evidence status.
- Prefer court records, official inquiries, archival documents and reputable investigative reporting. Distinguish allegations, charges, convictions and findings.
- Separate verified facts from claims, contested interpretations, reconstructions and speculation. Never fabricate quotations, evidence, photographs or case details.
- Record the date, jurisdiction and relevant context for legal outcomes. Update entries when convictions are overturned or new findings emerge.
- Link to sources rather than copying copyrighted material. Record rights and permissions for any reused images or documents.

## Harm reduction and privacy
- Do not expose nonpublic identifying details of victims, witnesses, children or private individuals.
- Use content notices and restrained thumbnails for graphic material. No sensationalized autoplay, gore-as-marketing, or offender glorification.
- Never publish operational instructions for violence, abuse or evading law enforcement.
- A paywall does not make otherwise inappropriate or unlawful content acceptable. Graphic exhibits require separate editorial and legal review, rights clearance, and a clear research justification.
- Offer corrections and removal-request channels before launch.

## Entry lifecycle
1. Proposed — topic suggested, not verified.
2. Researching — sources gathered, claims under review.
3. Verified — evidence checked and attribution complete.
4. Published — editorial review passed.
5. Corrected/withdrawn — publicly document material changes.

## Release gates
- No public factual article without provenance and review.
- No membership billing until clear terms, cancellation, refund and privacy workflows have been tested.
- No public deployment or paid integrations without explicit owner approval.
